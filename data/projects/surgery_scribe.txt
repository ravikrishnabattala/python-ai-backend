Project:
Surgery Scribe Validation

Focus:
Validation of an AI-powered surgery documentation workflow.

Application:
Surgery Scribe

Testing flow:

Original surgery note
    ↓
TTS audio
    ↓
Reference text
    ↓
Scribe STT
    ↓
AI Summary

Testing includes comparing generated speech-to-text and AI-generated summaries against reference information.

Validation areas:
- Speech-to-text accuracy
- AI summary accuracy
- Section-level validation
- Missing information
- Incorrect information
- Defect tracking
- Regression testing

Testing artifacts include:
- Comprehensive TKA Surgery reference text
- STT output
- AI Summary
- Validation results
- Issue tracking
- Severity
- Impact

The application was tested through the Scribe environment and included validation of AI-generated outputs against reference content.
